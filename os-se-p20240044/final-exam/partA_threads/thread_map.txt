--- /proc/<PID>/status | grep -i threads ---
Threads: 5

Interpretation: PID column is identical (1446921) for the process and all 4 worker
LWPs; the right-most numeric column (5th field shown by ps -eLf) differs per row —
that is each thread's distinct kernel-scheduled LWP id. This confirms Linux's 1:1
user-thread to kernel-thread (LWP) model: 4 pthread_create() calls produced 4
separate kernel-schedulable entities, all sharing the same PID/address space.
